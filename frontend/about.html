About the project

A college social internship project on AI ethics

FairLens was built as a social internship project focused on AI Ethics — Bias Detection: making the abstract idea of "algorithmic fairness" concrete enough for students, and anyone affected by automated decisions, to inspect for themselves.

Objectives

  • Demonstrate, with real calculations, how bias shows up in a dataset's outcomes across groups.
  • Make core fairness metrics — selection rate difference, demographic parity, disparate impact, equal opportunity — understandable without a statistics background.
  • Give students and educators a hands-on tool to test their own datasets, not just read about fairness in the abstract.
  • Keep every number transparent and reproducible, with the formula behind the fairness score fully disclosed.

Social impact

Automated decisions increasingly shape access to credit, jobs, healthcare, and education. Most people affected by these systems have no way to check whether they were treated fairly. FairLens is a small step toward that kind of accountability — a free, open way to run the same kind of fairness check a data science team would run internally, using a dataset you choose.

Scope & limits

What FairLens is, and isn't

What it is

An educational prototype that computes real, transparent fairness statistics over a dataset you provide, with a documented and auditable scoring method.

What it isn't

FairLens is not a certified legal, regulatory, medical, or financial compliance tool, and its composite fairness score should not be treated as an official audit result.
